Current Regulation – Article ○ (Appeal Request by the Perpetrating Student) If the perpetrating student or their guardian has an objection to "any measures" imposed by the Autonomous Committee, they may file an appeal with the Metropolitan or Provincial Student Disciplinary Adjustment Committee within 15 days from the date the measure was received. Proposed Amendment – Article ○ (Appeal Request by the Perpetrating Student) If the perpetrating student or their guardian has an objection to a "transfer or expulsion measure" imposed by the Autonomous Committee, they may file an appeal with the Metropolitan or Provincial Student Disciplinary Adjustment Committee within 15 days from the date the measure was received.
