## Original Question

There is a debate about which method should be chosen to design an artificial intelligence robot that predicts human behavior. Alice: People believe that human behavior can be successfully predicted through an understanding of humans’ internal states. However, we do not know exactly what method is needed to understand internal states that cannot be directly observed. Therefore, a way to predict human behavior without understanding internal states is needed. The method we can take in this case is to design the AI robot so that it can discover sophisticated patterns on its own, using big data, regarding which actions humans take in given situations. Bob: Alice’s method cannot be seen as one that can successfully predict human behavior. Human behavior, which is based on countless 'what if...' assumptions, requires a different approach in order to be predicted accurately. To improve the chances of successful prediction, it is necessary to pre-input the possible behaviors in given situations. All humans have a tendency to act according to social norms to avoid disadvantages. Therefore, if information about norms restricting human actions is entered when designing the AI robot, it will increase the success rate of predicting human behavior. Charlie: Even if we follow the methods proposed by Alice and Bob, it is difficult to predict human behavior successfully. Human actions are caused by various internal states. Thus, even if we apply both Alice's and Bob’s methods, predictions can still be wrong. Sometimes humans, even when aware of which actions are disadvantageous to themselves, do not follow norms due to stronger internal desires. Therefore, even if the design process becomes complex and inefficient, it should be designed to predict human behavior by considering not only normative information but also internal states such as intentions and desires. <question> Which of the following analyses about the debate is/are correct? Choose all that apply from <statements>. <statements> (a) Alice agrees, but Charlie does not, that if the AI robot cannot understand the internal states of humans, then it is impossible to predict human behavior. (b) Alice and Bob agree that there are patterns in human behavior in specific situations. (c) Bob and Charlie agree that it is necessary to consider normative information in order to predict human behavior. <choices> ① (a) ② (b) ③ (a), (c) ④ (b), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 4

Only (b) and (c) are correct, so the answer is ④.
