# Question 2021_35

## Original Question

<Theory> The concept of A consists of the characteristics that define A, that is, the characteristics that constitute the necessary and sufficient conditions for something to be A. For example, if the necessary and sufficient condition for an entity to be a “bachelor” is to be an unmarried man, then for an entity to be a bachelor, he must be both unmarried and male, and all entities who are both unmarried and male are bachelors. In this case, the concept of bachelor is composed of the characteristics of being unmarried and being male. If someone possesses the concept of A, they would use the concept of A to determine whether a specific entity belongs to category A or not. The time it takes to determine, using the concept of A, whether an entity falls under A is the sum of the time required to check whether that entity possesses each of the defining characteristics of A. <Experiment> Scientists presented participants with various kinds of animals, and asked them to perform a task to judge whether each animal was a bird. After posing questions like “Is x a bird?” they measured the time taken for participants to answer. The results showed that the average response time to say that a sparrow is a bird was 0.4 seconds, while for a penguin, it was 1.4 seconds on average. <question> Which of the following, as found among <statements>, is an appropriate evaluation of <Theory>? <statements> (a) If participants did not possess the concept of bird and answered based on the feel or impression of the words “sparrow” and “penguin,” then the results of <Experiment> do not weaken <Theory>. (b) If the time taken to confirm whether a sparrow and a penguin possess each characteristic that constitutes the concept of bird differs, then the results of <Experiment> weaken <Theory>. (c) If people who think that the concept of human is defined as “rational animal” take less time to judge whether an entity is a human than to judge whether the same entity is an animal, <Theory> is weakened. <choices> ① (a) ② (b) ③ (a), (c) ④ (b), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 3

Therefore, among the <choices>, only (a) and (c) are appropriate evaluations. The correct answer is ③.
