## Original Rationale

**Preliminaries**

The hypothesis presented is that "women will display greater creativity in environments where they work separately from men rather than in environments where they work together with men," and an `<Experiment>` is provided to test this. In the `<Experiment>`, the group where female singers collaborate in singing with male singers and male musicians serves as the control group, while the group where female singers perform separately from the male singer and musicians serves as the experimental group.

**Statement (a) Rationale**

If ⓐ is true, then female singers will achieve higher creativity scores when not collaborating with men compared to when they do collaborate. Therefore, if the female singers' scores in the experimental group are higher than in the control group, it means their scores were higher when not working with men, which supports ⓐ. Thus, (a) is a correct evaluation.

**Statement (b) Rationale**

For example, suppose the results showed that female singers had higher creative expression scores than male singers in the control group, that the scores of female and male singers were similar in the experimental group, and that the male singers' scores in the control and experimental groups were comparable. Even though the total score for all female singers exceeds that of all male singers, this could actually mean that female singers performed better when collaborating with men, which would oppose ⓐ. Therefore, the statement "the total score of all female singers was higher than that of all male singers" does not necessarily support ⓐ. Thus, (b) is not a correct evaluation.

**Statement (c) Rationale**

For instance, suppose the male singers' scores in the control and experimental groups are similar. If female singers scored slightly lower than the male singers in the control group, but outperformed the male singers in the experimental group, this means the female singers' scores were higher in the experimental group than in the control group, which supports ⓐ, not weakens it. Thus, it cannot be said that "if the female singers' scores in the control group were slightly lower than those of the male singers, but in the experimental group the female singers' scores were higher than those of the male singers, ⓐ is weakened." (c) is not a correct evaluation.

**Answer**

Therefore, only (a) provides a correct evaluation, so the answer is ①.

> According to the rationale, only statement (a) is correct.

**`<question>`** Which of the following in `<statements>` correctly evaluates the `<Experiment>`?

**`<statements>`**

### MODEL

The correct evaluation of the experiment is:

**(c)** If the female singers' scores in the control group were slightly lower than those of the male singers, but in the experimental group the female singers' scores were higher than those of the male singers, ⓐ is weakened.

**Explanation:**

The experiment aims to test the hypothesis that women will display greater creativity in environments where they work separately from men rather than environments where they work together with men. The experimental group, where the female singer records her part separately from the male singer and the musicians, is intended to allow the female singer to express her creativity without the influence of the male singer and the musicians.

If the female singers' scores were higher in the experimental group than in the control group (where they worked together with the male singer and the musicians), it would support the hypothesis (ⓐ). However, if the female singers' scores were higher in the experimental group but only because their scores in the control group were lower, it would suggest that the female singers are more creative when working alone, but it would not necessarily mean that they are more creative when working with men. This would weaken the hypothesis, making it less likely to be supported.

The other options are incorrect because:

- **(a)** If the female singers' scores were higher in the experimental group, it would support the hypothesis, regardless of their scores in the control group.
- **(b)** The total score of all female singers being higher than that of all male singers does not directly relate to the hypothesis being tested. The hypothesis is focused on the relative creativity of female singers in different environments, not on the overall performance of female versus male singers.
