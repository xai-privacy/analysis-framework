## Original question

Let us ask a participant in a bet to choose between $1 and $10, instructing them to make a choice that they will later regret. The participant is informed in advance that only if they make a regretful choice will they be awarded an additional $100. Choosing the option that yields more money is considered to be the rational choice, for which there will be no regret; any other choice is considered a regretful one. At first glance, "ⓐ the rational choice in this bet" appears to be selecting $1. This is because giving up $10 to take $1 would be a regretful choice, and thus the $100 would be awarded. However, if this is the case, choosing $1 would no longer be a regretful choice, so the $100 would not actually be paid out. Although the choice is made only once, whether it is regarded as a regretful choice changes continuously, causing the payment or non-payment of the $100 to endlessly oscillate between the two. The same thing happens if $10 is chosen. Since choosing $10 instead of $1 is not a regretful choice, the $100 is not paid. In that case, however, this choice becomes a regretful one, so the $100 should then be paid; but then, the choice of $10 again becomes not a regretful choice. Here too, the $100 endlessly oscillates between being awarded and not awarded. Thus, it is impossible to say what the rational choice is in this bet. Let us examine the structure of this bet, following the flow from the point at which one contemplates what choice to make (point 0) through to the point at which one looks back on one’s choice (point 2). The moment the choice between $1 and $10 is made, point 1, lies between points 0 and 2. The intention behind this choice is to experience regret at point 2. The object of this intention is a future event that has not yet occurred. In contrast, regret concerns the past, which has already happened. The participant in this bet intends at point 0 to regret at point 2 the choice made at point 1. But precisely because this intention is realized, the regretful choice made at point 1 becomes a non-regretful choice. And this process repeats endlessly.
