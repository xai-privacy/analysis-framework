# Question 2024_24

## Original Question

<Hypothesis> People judge Action X, which is performed under situational pressure, and the morality of the actor as follows: - If X is bad, they judge it as voluntary; if X is good, they judge it as coerced.- If X is judged as voluntary, people judge the actor’s morality based on X, but if X is judged as coerced, they don’t use X to infer morality.<Experiment> 100 participants are divided into Group 1 and Group 2. Group 1 reads Passage 1 and Group 2 reads Passage 2, respectively.Passage 1: Alice and Bob encounter a homeless person. Alice tells Bob to give all the money he has to the homeless person. Bob gives all the money he had to the homeless person. → Since the action is good, it is considered coerced. No judgment of morality.Passage 2: Alice and Bob encounter a homeless person. Alice tells Bob to take the homeless person’s money. Bob takes the homeless person’s money. → Since the action is bad, it is considered voluntary. Judgment of morality.After reading the passage, each group is asked whether Bob’s action was voluntary or coerced, and whether Bob is moral or not. <question> Which of the following evaluations about the text is/are correct according to <statements>? <statements> (a) If most people in Group 1 who answered that Bob’s action was coerced also answered that Bob is moral, the <hypothesis> is weakened.(b) If most people in Group 1 answered that Bob’s action was coerced, but most in Group 2 answered that Bob’s action was voluntary, the <hypothesis> is weakened.(c) If most people in Group 1 answered that they don’t know whether Bob is moral or not, but most in Group 2 answered that Bob is immoral, the <hypothesis> is weakened. <choices> ① (a) ② (c) ③ (a), (b) ④ (b), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 1

Only (a) is correct, so the answer is ①.

## LLM Response

Answer-③ (a), (b).

 Rationale 1. If most people in Group 1 who answered that Bob’s action was coerced also answered that Bob is moral, it suggests that the text implies that coerced actions are judged as moral. This contradicts the hypothesis that coerced actions are not used to infer morality.

 Rationale 2. If most people in Group 1 answered that Bob’s action was coerced, but most in Group 2 answered that Bob’s action was voluntary, it suggests that the text implies that coerced actions are judged as moral, which also contradicts the hypothesis.

 Rationale 3. The hypothesis assumes that if most people in Group 1 answered that Bob’s action was coerced, they would also answer that Bob is immoral, not that they don't know. If most in Group 2 answered that Bob is immoral, it is consistent with the hypothesis that if an action is judged as voluntary, people judge the actor's morality based on the action, but if an action is judged as coerced, they don't use the action to infer morality.
