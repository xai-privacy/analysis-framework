# Question 2022_31

## Original Question

An egoistic individual only considers the utility gained from their own consumption. If so, why does the act of donation exist? It is because of altruism, which takes into account not only the utility gained through personal consumption but also the utility of the recipient of the donation. The hypothesis that claims humans consider both their own utility and that of the recipient is called the ⓐ Pure Altruism Hypothesis. Under this hypothesis, the donor first determines the total amount of donation needed by the recipient. If the donor learns that the recipient has received a certain amount from other donors, the donor reduces their donation by exactly that amount. On the other hand, there is an argument that the emotional utility obtained from the act of donating itself also plays an important role in donation behavior. This is called the ⓑ Impure Altruism Hypothesis.
