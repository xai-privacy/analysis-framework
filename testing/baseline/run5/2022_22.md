# Question 2022_22

## Original Question

Predicates related to people’s tastes, such as “delicious” or “fun,” are called taste predicates. Regarding sentences containing taste predicates, Alice and Bob had the following debate.Alice: The sentence “Gopchang is delicious” always grammatically includes the hidden phrase “to x.” Here, ‘x’ is a variable whose value is filled by the speaker in a given context. For example, if Jiwoo, who finds gopchang delicious, says, “Gopchang is delicious,” Jiwoo’s statement expresses the proposition <Gopchang is delicious to Jiwoo>, which is a true assertion. On the other hand, if Youngho, who does not like gopchang, says the same sentence, Youngho’s statement expresses the different proposition <Gopchang is delicious to Youngho>, which becomes a false assertion.Bob: When Jiwoo says “Gopchang is delicious,” Youngho can respond by saying “No, gopchang is not delicious!” and then the two would start to argue about it. But if Alice’s view is correct, then Jiwoo merely expresses the proposition <Gopchang is delicious to Jiwoo> and Youngho is expressing the denial of a different proposition, so they are not really engaging in a genuine dispute. However, clearly in such situations the two can have a real argument, which shows there is a serious problem with Alice’s view. To resolve this, we should take the sentence “Gopchang is delicious” to express the proposition <Gopchang is delicious>, regardless of who says it. <question> Which of the following analyses regarding this debate is/are correct as given in <statements>?<statements> (a) According to Alice, the statements “Gopchang is delicious” made by people who enjoy gopchang all express the same proposition, but this proposition is different from the one expressed by statements “Gopchang is delicious” made by people who do not enjoy gopchang. (b) If Youngho dislikes gopchang, then according to Alice, Youngho’s statement “Gopchang is delicious” cannot be true, but according to Bob, it can be true. (c) Bob’s argument assumes that disagreement about the truth value of the same proposition is a necessary condition for their debate to be a genuine dispute. <choices> ① (a) ② (b) ③ (a), (c) ④ (b), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 4

Therefore, only (b) and (c) are the correct analyses, making ④ the correct answer.

## LLM Response

Answer-③.

1. Statement (a) is true as Alice states that people who enjoy gopchang express the same proposition but one who doesn't enjoy it expresses a different proposition.
2. Statement (b) is false because if Youngho dislikes gopchang, then his statement "Gopchang is delicious" cannot be true according to Alice but according to Bob it can be true because they can still have an argument about its truth value.
