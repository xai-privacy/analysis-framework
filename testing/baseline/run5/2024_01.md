# Question 2024_01

## Original Question

<Opinion> A: A tort is an act that destroys the originally just or equitable state of affairs. Therefore, the law of torts is a system designed to restore the original state that was destroyed by a tort and to remedy the victim. The aim of restoration in tort law is a demand based on justice or ethics, not because it contributes to the promotion of social utility or is functionally useful. Furthermore, unless the victim is completely restored to the state prior to the tort from the victim's perspective, rather than from the viewpoint of the perpetrator or a third party (including the social community), true remedy for the victim has failed. B: The essence of a tort lies in the fact that it is an unlawful act committed intentionally or negligently by a person. Therefore, the law of torts should aim not only at restoring the loss caused by a tort but also at preventing tortious conduct. Tort law should provide behavioral guidelines for members of society and give incentives to promote desirable behavior. Preventive messages should be addressed not only to the perpetrator, but to all members of the community, including the perpetrator. When deciding what kind of message to deliver, it is necessary to seriously consider what constitutes the greatest good for the community. <question> Which of the following in <statements> is correct as an assessment of the <Opinion>? <statements> (a) In a case where damages involved the destruction of property and the cost of repair exceeded the replacement value of the item, if a court in country X ruled that the perpetrator must compensate the victim for the full repair cost, this would weaken A. (b) In a case where an employee transferred company C’s trade secrets to a competitor for profit, but the company suffered no actual damage, if a court in country X ruled that the illicit gains should nonetheless be compensated to the company as damages, this would strengthen B. (c) In a case where someone repeatedly used demeaning expressions to defame another, but the victim forgave the offender, if a court in country X ruled compensation should be paid not to the victim but to a third party on the grounds that tolerating such demeaning expressions would undermine free debate in society, this would weaken A and strengthen B. <choices> ① (a) ② (c) ③ (a), (b) ④ (b), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 4

Of the above, only (b) and (c) are correct, so the answer is ④.

## LLM Response

Answer-③

1. A is correct in stating that a tort destroys the originally just or equitable state of affairs, and the primary aim of tort law is restoration. However, B's assertion that tort law should not only restore losses but also prevent tortious conduct is also accurate. Preventive measures can promote desirable behavior and protect members of society.

2. If the court in country X ruled that the perpetrator must compensate the victim for the full repair cost in a case involving property damage (option a), it would weaken A, as A suggests that the law of torts should only aim to restore the loss caused by a tort, not the full cost of repair.

3. In the case of an employee transferring company C's trade secrets to a competitor for profit (option b), if the court ruled that the illicit gains should be compensated to the company as damages, it would strengthen B. This is because such a ruling would not only compensate the company for its loss but also deter similar behavior in the future, thereby promoting desirable behavior and social utility.

4. If the court ruled compensation should be paid not to the victim but to a third party on the grounds that tolerating demeaning expressions would undermine free debate in society (option c), it would both weaken A and strengthen B. By focusing on the broader social implications and the potential consequences of tolerating certain behaviors, this ruling would align with B's suggestion that tort law should provide behavioral guidelines for members of society and promote desirable behavior.
