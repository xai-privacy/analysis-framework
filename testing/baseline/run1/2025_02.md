# Question 2025_02

## Original Question

In the face of the growing severity of school violence, as public opinion has risen that the current regulations are insufficient to protect victims and provide prompt remedies, a proposed amendment has been submitted to the National Assembly. Current Regulation – Article ○ (Appeal Request by the Perpetrating Student) If the perpetrating student or their guardian has an objection to "any measures" imposed by the Autonomous Committee, they may file an appeal with the Metropolitan or Provincial Student Disciplinary Adjustment Committee within 15 days from the date the measure was received. Proposed Amendment – Article ○ (Appeal Request by the Perpetrating Student) If the perpetrating student or their guardian has an objection to a "transfer or expulsion measure" imposed by the Autonomous Committee, they may file an appeal with the Metropolitan or Provincial Student Disciplinary Adjustment Committee within 15 days from the date the measure was received. The following <Opinion> was presented regarding this matter. <Opinion> Parents have the right to participate in the educational process of their underage children, so when the school imposes a disadvantageous measure on a student, they also have the right to express their opinion on the matter. The proposed amendment, by allowing appeals only in cases of transfer and expulsion, "infringes ⓐ upon such rights of the parents." Furthermore, since the amendment permits appeals only to perpetrating students who have received transfer or expulsion measures, it "results in the unjust discrimination of other perpetrating students and their guardians who have received other measures ⓑ." Therefore, the current regulation should be maintained. <question> Which of the following analyses about the <Opinion> is/are correct in the <statements> below? <statements> (a) If legal remedies are available by other means for measures for which appeals are not permitted, ⓐ is mitigated. (b) If the harm suffered by perpetrating students due to transfer or expulsion cannot be restored even if such measures are overturned in a subsequent lawsuit—unlike with other measures—then ⓑ is weakened. (c) If it is the responsibility of schools and society to ensure that all perpetrating students are given the opportunity to appeal and thereby grow into exemplary members of society, then ⓐ is weakened and ⓑ is strengthened. <choices> ① (b) ② (c) ③ (a), (b) ④ (a), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 1

Only (b) is a correct analysis from the given choices, so the correct answer is ①.

## LLM Response

Answer-③. Rationale 1. The Opinions states that parents have the right to participate in the educational process of their underage children, so they have the right to express their opinion on the matter. However, if legal remedies are available by other means for measures for which appeals are not permitted, the harm caused to the parents is mitigated, and the right to express their opinion is less significant. 2. The Opinions argues that the proposed amendment infringes upon the rights of parents, but it does not explicitly state that the harm suffered by perpetrating students cannot be restored even if such measures are overturned in a subsequent lawsuit. Therefore, statement (b) is not directly supported by the Opinions.
