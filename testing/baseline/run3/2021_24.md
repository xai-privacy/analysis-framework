# Question 2021_24

## Original Question

The roles of nature and culture in human development are a matter of debate, with opinions divided as follows.<Opinions>Alice: I believe that in order to understand human development, we must start from human nature. This is because there are clear differences between groups, and especially biological characteristics play a decisive role in explaining intergroup differences. In addition, many researchers have proven that differences in intelligence quotient between individuals are due to genetic factors.Bob: The fact that intelligence quotients among groups divided by race, gender, etc. show almost no difference is an undeniable fact accepted in the scientific community. Because the human species is so homogeneous, I think that insisting on intergroup differences based on biological criteria stems from a misguided motivation to uphold unequal social hierarchies. Social hierarchy only limits human variability; humans can be shaped differently depending on culture or social environment. <question> Which of the following in <Statements> properly evaluates the <Opinions>? <statements> (a) Research results indicating that in every society throughout history, crime rates have been overwhelmingly higher in a particular group—males between the ages of 15 and 25—weaken Alice's argument.(b) Research results that various social reforms aimed at fairly developing the abilities of all members of society have failed do not strengthen Bob's argument.(c) Research results showing that, as a result of improving English education programs, the English test scores of most elementary school students increased significantly compared to before the improvement, strengthen Alice's argument and weaken Bob's argument. <choices> ① (a) ② (b) ③ (a), (c) ④ (b), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 2

Therefore, only (b) in the <Choices> is an appropriate evaluation, so the correct answer is ②.

## LLM Response

Answer-③ (a), (c)

Rationale 1. Option (c) is correct because it strengthens Alice's argument by demonstrating that biological characteristics, such as intelligence quotient, can be influenced by the environment. This undermines Bob's argument that social environment plays a significant role in shaping human development.

Rationale 2. Option (a) is correct because it challenges Alice's assumption that human nature is the primary determinant of human development. If crime rates are indeed higher in a particular group, it could suggest that environmental factors, such as socialization or socioeconomic conditions, play a significant role in shaping human behavior, which would weaken Alice's argument.
