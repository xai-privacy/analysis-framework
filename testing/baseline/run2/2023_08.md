# Question 2023_08

## Original Question

"Mediation" refers to facilitating convenience between a person and their counterpart by brokering certain matters. Article A of Country X’s "Criminal Law" punishes specific acts of mediation.Alice: Even people without the status of public officials can use personal influence such as alumni or regional ties to affect the duties of public officials, so Article A punishes mediation by such people as well.Bob: Because protecting public trust in the execution of public officials' duties is important, Article A punishes those who pursue their own interests under the guise of mediation concerning a public official’s duty, regardless of whether they actually performed mediation.Charlie: There is no need to prohibit bona fide mediation acts, so Article A prohibits mediation concerning a public official’s duty undertaken for one’s own interests. In this case, for preventive effect, Article A also compels those involved in mediation not to retain any property acquired in connection with the mediation. <question> Which of the following most appropriately describes the content of Article A, satisfying all the matters mentioned by Alice, Bob, and Charlie? <statements>(1) Anyone who receives or promises to receive money or benefits in connection with mediation regarding matters pertaining to a public official’s duties shall be punished by imprisonment for not more than five years or a fine not exceeding 10 million won.(2) Anyone who receives or promises to receive money or benefits and mediates regarding matters pertaining to a public official’s duties shall be punished by imprisonment for not more than five years, and any property acquired thereby shall be confiscated.(3) Any public official who, by using their position, receives or promises to receive money or benefits in connection with mediation regarding matters pertaining to another public official’s duties shall be punished by imprisonment for not more than five years or a fine not exceeding 10 million won.(4) Anyone who receives or promises to receive money or benefits in connection with mediation regarding matters pertaining to a public official’s duties shall be punished by imprisonment for not more than five years or a fine not exceeding 10 million won, and any property acquired thereby shall be confiscated.(5) Anyone who provides or expresses the intention to provide money or benefits in connection with mediation regarding matters pertaining to a public official’s duties shall be punished by imprisonment for not more than five years or disqualification for not more than seven years, and any property acquired thereby shall be confiscated. <choices> ①(1) ②(2) ③(3) ④(4) ⑤(5)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 4

Only (4) is the correct analysis among the <choices>, so the answer is ④.

## LLM Response

Answer-④. Rationale 1. Article A is designed to address the misuse of personal influence or regional ties to affect public officials' duties, which is reflected in statements made by both Alice and Bob. Statement (4) fully incorporates elements from both statements, including punishment for individuals involved in mediation, confiscation of acquired property, and punishment for public officials who misuse their influence. Statement (4) also aligns with Charlie's perspective that there should be no prohibition on genuine mediation acts.
