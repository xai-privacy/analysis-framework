# Question 2024_37

## Original Question

Mendelian genetics is based on the idea that ‘ⓐit does not matter whether a particular gene comes from the sperm or the egg.’ However, in mammals, it has been found that for some genes, it is important whether they came from the sperm or the egg, and only the allele derived exclusively from either the sperm or the egg is expressed. This phenomenon can occur due to DNA methylation, a process in which a methyl group (–CH₃) attaches to DNA. During spermatogenesis and oogenesis, existing DNA methylation is reset, and new, sex-specific DNA methylation occurs. The Surani research team experimentally demonstrated the idea that ‘ⓑthe activity of a gene at a specific region of DNA can differ depending on whether this region was inherited from either parent.’ The research team created genetically modified mice with a specific DNA inserted that makes it easy to measure the degree of methylation. They investigated the methylation level of this DNA in the offspring of these mice, and found that the DNA inherited from the mother was highly methylated, whereas the DNA inherited from the father was methylated to a lesser extent. DNA methylation is related to the regulation of gene expression. Regulation of gene expression by DNA methylation can occur either through ‘ⓒa mechanism in which the methyl group interferes with the binding of a regulatory protein to the DNA’ or ‘ⓓa mechanism in which a regulatory protein binds to the DNA via the methyl group.’ At this time, the regulatory protein can either activate or suppress the expression of the gene. During the early embryonic development of mice, only gene X derived from the father is expressed, and the gene X derived from the mother is not, because a specific regulatory protein binds only to the DNA of the maternally inherited gene X. <question> Which of the following evaluations regarding the above passage is/are appropriate as stated in <statements>? <statements> (a) The experimental results of the Surani research team reinforce ⓐ. (b) If Prader-Willi syndrome appears in a child only when a particular chromosomal abnormality is inherited from the father but not when inherited from the mother, then ⓑ is reinforced. (c) If only the DNA of gene X inherited from the mother is methylated, then the regulation of gene X's expression corresponds to ⓓ among the mechanisms ⓒ and ⓓ. <choices> ① (a) ② (b) ③ (a), (c) ④ (b), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 4

Only options (b) and (c) are appropriate evaluations based on the passage, so the answer is ④.

## LLM Response

If Prader-Willi syndrome appears only when the particular chromosomal abnormality is inherited from the father but not from the mother, it reinforces the idea that ⓑ can be true. 3. If only the DNA of gene X inherited from the mother is methylated, it matches with mechanism ⓓ, where a regulatory protein binds to the DNA via the methyl group.
