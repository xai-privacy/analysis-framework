# Question 2021_25

## Original Question

In social sciences, classical experimental research may not be able to generalize experimental results to the real world. For example, suppose an experimental study was conducted to determine whether ‘watching a movie where a Black person appears as a hero’ (the experimental stimulus) reduces ‘the degree of negative prejudice against Black people’, and the following findings were observed. First, in the experimental group that received the experimental stimulus, the degree of prejudice was lower in the post-test than in the pre-test. Second, in the control group that did not receive the experimental stimulus, there was no change in the degree of prejudice between the pre-test and the post-test. In this case, it can be considered that watching the movie reduced the degree of prejudice among participants in the experimental group. However, it cannot be asserted with certainty that the same effect would appear if the movie was watched by people in their everyday lives. This is because in experiments, participants may already have become sensitive to the issue of prejudice against Black people as a result of the pre-test. To solve this problem, an <experimental design> that adds an experiment without a pre-test must be implemented. If, through this, experimental results are obtained that demonstrate ‘ⓐ that watching the movie reduced the degree of prejudice’, the possibility of generalization can be increased.<Experimental Design>• Group 1: Pre-test → Experimental stimulus → Post-test.• Group 2: Pre-test → Post-test.• Group 3: No pre-test → Experimental stimulus → Post-test.• Group 4: No pre-test → Post-test.All participants in Groups 1 to 4 were randomly selected from the population. <question> Which of the following cannot be included in the experimental results that demonstrate ⓐ?<choices>① In Group 1, the degree of prejudice in the post-test was lower than the degree of prejudice in the pre-test.② The degree of prejudice in the post-test of Group 1 was lower than the degree of prejudice in the post-test of Group 2.③ The degree of prejudice in the post-test of Group 3 was lower than the degree of prejudice in the pre-test of Group 2.④ The degree of prejudice in the post-test of Group 3 was lower than the degree of prejudice in the post-test of Group 4.⑤ The degree of prejudice in the post-test of Group 4 was lower than the degree of prejudice in the post-test of Group 1.

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 5

Only ⑤ is the correct analysis among the <choices>, so the answer is ⑤.

## LLM Response

Answer-③. Rationale 3. The statement cannot be included in the experimental results that demonstrate that watching the movie reduced the degree of prejudice because Group 3 has no pre-test. It is impossible to compare the degree of prejudice in the post-test of Group 3 with the pre-test of Group 2 or Group 4.
