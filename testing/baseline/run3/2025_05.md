# Question 2025_05

## Original Question

To control a contract concluded by the parties’ free will, there must be a justified reason. There exist two claims about this justification: Claim A, which states that it is to correct market failure arising from information asymmetry, and Claim B, which states that it is to provide protection from a power imbalance. <Claims> A: Information asymmetry causes market failure by creating a situation in which relevant circumstances significant for the conclusion and content of the contract are unequally distributed between parties. The risk of qualitative deterioration of products resulting from market failure is borne entirely by the party with inferior information. In order to correct market failure caused by information asymmetry, contracts must be controlled. Information asymmetry can be resolved simply by providing the relevant information to the counterparty. Information already known by the other party or information such as market price is considered as having been provided. B: In the hands of the strong, a contract becomes a powerful weapon, while in the hands of the weak, it is a blunt tool. The right to self-determination in contracts is guaranteed only when the parties have equal bargaining power. If one party is a minor and the other is an adult, or if only one party is a state, as in cases where contracts are concluded in a state of power imbalance, only one party’s self-determination is protected, so the contract must be controlled. (a). If Alice, an adult, hands over a manual containing all information about her property to Bob, a minor who has graduated from college, and then enters into a sales contract with him, the control of this contract is not justified by A, but is justified by B. (b). If Charlie, a minor, sells his bicycle at a price much lower than the market price to David, an adult whom he has never met before through an online brokerage platform, the control of this contract is justified by A but not justified by B. (c). If adult Eve and State X both know all information regarding a certain piece of land and then enter into a sales contract regarding that land, the control of this contract is justified neither by A nor by B. <question> Which of the following, among the statements in <statements>, are correct evaluations of the <claims> above? <choices> ① (a) ② (c) ③ (a), (b) ④ (b), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 1

Of the options, only (a) is a correct evaluation; thus, the answer is ①.

## LLM Response

Answer-④ Rationale 1. The claim in A emphasizes the correction of market failure caused by information asymmetry. This situation does not apply to scenario (c), where all parties possess equal information. 2. The claim in B focuses on the protection of the rights of the weaker party when there is a power imbalance. In scenario (c), where the contract is entered into between equal parties, there is no power imbalance, so the right to self-determination is not violated.
