# Question 2022_31

## Original Question

An egoistic individual only considers the utility gained from their own consumption. If so, why does the act of donation exist? It is because of altruism, which takes into account not only the utility gained through personal consumption but also the utility of the recipient of the donation. The hypothesis that claims humans consider both their own utility and that of the recipient is called the ⓐ Pure Altruism Hypothesis. Under this hypothesis, the donor first determines the total amount of donation needed by the recipient. If the donor learns that the recipient has received a certain amount from other donors, the donor reduces their donation by exactly that amount. On the other hand, there is an argument that the emotional utility obtained from the act of donating itself also plays an important role in donation behavior. This is called the ⓑ Impure Altruism Hypothesis. The impure altruism hypothesis considers not only the donor’s utility and the recipient’s utility, as in the pure altruism hypothesis, but also the donor’s own emotional utility.To test the two hypotheses above, the following experiment is independently conducted with many participants.<Experiment> Each participant is provided with the table below and must decide all of a~f. Afterwards, in one randomly chosen situation from A~F, each participant receives the corresponding income and donates the amount they determined.<Table>- In Situation A, the participant’s income is 40, their donation amount is a, and the charity’s donation is 4.- In Situation B, the participant’s income is 40, their donation amount is b, and the charity’s donation is 10.- In Situation C, the participant’s income is 40, their donation amount is c, and the charity’s donation is 28.- In Situation D, the participant’s income is 40, their donation amount is d, and the charity’s donation is 34.- In Situation E, the participant’s income is 46, their donation amount is e, and the charity’s donation is 4.- In Situation F, the participant’s income is 46, their donation amount is f, and the charity’s donation is 28. <question>  Choose all the correct evaluations regarding the text from the statements below. <statements> (a) If for most participants b = e − 6, then this strengthens ⓑ. (b) If for most participants e − a < f − c, then this strengthens ⓐ. (c) If for most participants 0 < a − 30 < b − 24 < c − 6 < d, then this strengthens ⓑ. <choices> ① (a) ② (c) ③ (a), (b) ④ (b), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 2

Because only (c) is a correct evaluation, the answer is ②.
