## Original Question

When we use the name “Napoleon,” which is the name of a historical figure, in everyday life, this name refers to the real person, Napoleon. However, in fictional works such as the novel War and Peace, in which Napoleon appears as a character, does the name “Napoleon” used there also refer to the real person Napoleon? We naturally have the intuition that it does.But let us imagine an extreme fictional work in which Napoleon appears as an Americano and goes on adventures with coffee friends.
