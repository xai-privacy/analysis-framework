Since Alice's observation felt clear to her, she did not take the simple step of going down to the ground floor to check the facts. Therefore, responsibility is not assigned to Alice. A states that in cases of believing or asserting something untrue as the truth, what is crucial is not a permissible level of negligence or cognitive error, as in the above example, but rather an active attitude of intentionally avoiding or refusing to acquire information.
