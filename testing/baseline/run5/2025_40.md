## Original Question

Hormones that are synthesized in one tissue, move to another tissue, and regulate physiological activity in that tissue can be classified into peptide hormones and steroid hormones. Most steroid hormones have low polarity and easily penetrate the cell membrane to act directly as signaling molecules inside the cell, whereas most peptide hormones have high polarity and cannot penetrate the cell membrane. Therefore, peptide hormones that cannot penetrate the cell membrane bind to receptors present on the cell surface and use a mechanism that activates intracellular signaling proteins through structural changes in these receptors. Animal steroid hormones penetrate the cell membrane easily, but the plant steroid hormone brassinolide (Br), which promotes plant growth, is known to be unable to penetrate the cell membrane easily, despite there being no significant difference between the cell membranes of animal cells and plant cells. <Experiment> is part of a study that elucidated the mechanism by which Br, similar to peptide hormones, utilizes cell surface receptors and intracellular signaling proteins. <Experiment> Among Arabidopsis mutants, mutant A with a defect only in the Br biosynthesis gene and mutant B with a defect only in the Br receptor gene were isolated. Both showed a growth-inhibited phenotype compared to the wild type. When Br was applied, the inhibited growth of A was not restored, but B’s growth was restored. A mutant C, which exhibited a growth-inhibited phenotype due solely to a mutation in the gene for the signaling protein that recognizes the structural change in the Br receptor, was also selected, and the phenotypic change after Br treatment was observed. <question> Which of the following analyses of the above passage is/are correct? Select all correct statements among those listed in <statements>: <statements> (a) If the difference in membrane permeability between animal and plant steroid hormones is determined only by polarity, Br would be more polar than animal steroid hormones. (b) <Experiment> supports the hypothesis that “A cannot synthesize Br, and B has a defect in the Br receptor.” (c) After Br treatment, B and C will show similar phenotypes. <choices> ① (a) ② (c) ③ (a), (b) ④ (b), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 1

Only (a) is correct, so the answer is ①.
