## Original Question

Hormones that are synthesized in one tissue, move to another tissue, and regulate physiological activity in that tissue can be classified into peptide hormones and steroid hormones. Most steroid hormones have low polarity and easily penetrate the cell membrane to act directly as signaling molecules inside the cell, whereas most peptide hormones have high polarity and cannot penetrate the cell membrane. Therefore, peptide hormones that cannot penetrate the cell membrane bind to receptors present on the cell surface and use a mechanism that activates intracellular signaling proteins through structural changes in these receptors. Animal steroid hormones penetrate the cell membrane easily, but the plant steroid hormone brassinolide (Br), which promotes plant growth, is known to be unable to penetrate the cell membrane easily, despite there being no significant difference between the cell membranes of animal cells and plant cells. <Experiment> is part of a study that elucidated the mechanism by which Br, similar to peptide hormones, utilizes cell surface receptors and intracellular signaling proteins. <Experiment> Among Arabidopsis mutants, mutant A with a defect only in the Br biosynthesis gene and mutant B with a defect only in the Br receptor gene were isolated. Both showed a growth-inhibited phenotype compared to the wild type. When Br was applied, the inhibited growth of A was not restored, but B’s growth was restored. A mutant C, which exhibited a growth-inhibited phenotype due solely to a mutation in the gene for the signaling protein that recognizes the structural change in the Br receptor, was also selected, and the phenotypic change after Br treatment was observed. <question> Which of the following analyses of the above passage is/are correct? Select all correct statements among those listed in <statements>: <statements> (a) If the difference in membrane permeability between animal and plant steroid hormones is determined only by polarity, Br would be more polar than animal steroid hormones. (b) <Experiment> supports the hypothesis that “A cannot synthesize Br, and B has a defect in the Br receptor.” (c) After Br treatment, B and C will show similar phenotypes. <choices> ① (a) ② (c) ③ (a), (b) ④ (b), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 1

Only (a) is correct, so the answer is ①.

## LLM Response

Answer-{③}. 

1. The solution to this question lies in understanding the mechanisms of how peptide hormones and steroid hormones interact with cells. It is established that most peptide hormones can’t penetrate the cell membrane due to their high polarity and thus bind to receptors on the cell surface and then activate intracellular signaling proteins. On the other hand, steroid hormones have low polarity and can penetrate the cell membrane, acting directly as signaling molecules inside the cell. 

2. The passage discusses an experiment conducted to study how plant steroid hormone Br works in plant cells. It highlights that despite Br being a steroid hormone like animal steroid hormones, it has a harder time penetrating the cell membrane and thus does not act like typical steroid hormones. Instead, it utilizes cell surface receptors and intracellular signaling proteins similar to peptide hormones.

3. In the experiment, two Arabidopsis mutants, A and B, were selected based on their mutations: A couldn’t synthesize Br, and B had a defect in the Br receptor. Both showed a growth-inhibited phenotype, and their growth was restored by Br treatment in different ways. Mutant C had a mutation in the signaling protein recognizing the structural change in the Br receptor and also displayed a growth-inhibited phenotype. 

4. Given the information provided about how Br works in plant cells and the experiment conducted, statement (c) is correct. After Br treatment, both B and C, which have defects in the Br receptor and in the signaling protein recognizing structural change in the Br receptor, respectively, will display similar phenotypes of having their growth restored due to Br. 

5. Statement (a) is incorrect because, according to the passage, the difficulty in Br penetrating the cell membrane is not primarily due to its polarity but rather some other characteristic of its structure. If the difference in membrane permeability were solely determined by polarity, animal steroid hormones would be less polar than Br because Br is known to have a harder time penetrating cell membranes compared to animal steroid hormones. 

6. Statement (b) is incorrect because the passage does not support the hypothesis that mutant A cannot synthesize Br and mutant B has a defect in the Br receptor. Instead, it supports the hypothesis that mutant A has a defect in the Br biosynthesis gene, and mutant B has a defect in the Br receptor.
