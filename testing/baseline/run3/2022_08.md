# Question 2022_08

## Original Question

[Regulation] Article 1 (Definition) “Child” refers to a minor. Article 2 (Physical Child Abuse) Anyone who commits an act of physical abuse by assaulting a child or causing harm to his or her physical health and development shall be punished by imprisonment for not more than 5 years. Article 3 (Sexual Child Abuse) Anyone who commits an act of sexual abuse causing sexual humiliation to a child shall be punished by imprisonment for not more than 6 years. <Review Opinions> Alice: Crimes of child abuse are distinct from general violent crimes in that the guardian, who has a duty of care, inflicts harm upon the person to be protected. Therefore, the child, as the person to be protected, should be excluded from being the subject of the act in Articles 2 and 3, and the subject should be limited to “adults” who are the guardians. Bob: The [Regulation] aims to thoroughly punish the perpetrators of abuse and thereby provide strong protection for children, who are the victims of various abusive acts. Therefore, the subject of the acts in Articles 2 and 3 should be retained as “anyone” as in the current regulation. Charlie: In relation to sexual acts, child victims cannot be treated in the same way as adult victims who possess sexual self-determination. Therefore, it is reasonable to delete the phrase “causing sexual humiliation” from Article 3. <question> Which of the following in <statements> correctly evaluates the <Review Opinions> regarding the [Regulation]? <statements> (a). The research finding that “recently, more minors are entering social relationships in which one minor is the guardian or supervisor of another minor” supports Alice’s opinion. (b). The research finding that “a significant number of perpetrators of child abuse were themselves victims of child abuse as children, so it is impossible to dichotomously distinguish between victims and perpetrators of child abuse” supports Bob’s opinion. (c). The research finding that “recently, cases where minors make sexual requests to other minors, involving the filming of videos, are increasing. Experiences of responding to such requests without any sense of shame or discomfort critically influence the later development of negative self-identity or distorted sexual awareness. Therefore, the need to punish sexual requests between minors as acts of abuse is great”—this research finding supports both Bob and Charlie’s opinions. <choices> ① (a) ② (c) ③ (a), (b) ④ (b), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 2

Since only (c) is a correct evaluation among the <choices>, the answer is ②.

## LLM Response

Answer-{④} Rationale 1. (a) does not correctly evaluate the Review Opinions. The research finding does not support Alice's opinion. Alice's opinion is based on the fact that a guardian is required to provide care and protection, but the research finding is about minors entering social relationships in which one minor is the guardian or supervisor of another minor. 2. (b) does not correctly evaluate the Review Opinions. The research finding does not support Bob's opinion. Bob's opinion is based on the idea that the regulation aims to thoroughly punish perpetrators of abuse, but the research finding is about the relationship between perpetrators and victims of child abuse. 3. (c) correctly evaluates the Review Opinions. The research finding supports both Bob's opinion and Charlie's opinion. The finding highlights the need to punish sexual requests between minors as acts of abuse, which aligns with Bob's view of the regulation's goal and Charlie's perspective on the need to protect minors from sexual abuse.
