# Question 2025_22

## Original Question

Alice, Bob, and Charlie formed a dance group called “Samshingi.” The following are discussions about the existence of the group. A: There is no such thing as a group. The only things that exist are the members of the group. For example, the statement “Samshingi performed” does not make sense; instead, one should say “Alice, Bob, and Charlie performed together.” B: Samshingi is a new entity distinct from the three individuals, but it is identical to the mere collection of Alice, Bob, and Charlie. Samshingi, as the collection of Alice, Bob, and Charlie, has the three as its parts and occupies a specific space. C: Even if the members change, Samshingi can continue to exist. Samshingi comes into existence only when the three form the group, but it is different from each individual or their mere collection. Samshingi should be seen not as something that occupies a particular space, but as an abstract entity. <question> Which of the following can be properly inferred from the above? Choose all correct statements from <statements> as they are. <statements> (a) Both A and B agree that only Alice, Bob, and Charlie exist, and that the group Samshingi does not exist. (b) Suppose that after the formation of Samshingi, Alice, Bob, and Charlie become interested in chess and form a chess club called “Checkmate.” B agrees, but C does not, that Samshingi and Checkmate are identical. (c) Both A and C agree that Samshingi continues to exist even if Alice is replaced by a new member. <choices> ① (a) ② (b) ③ (a), (c) ④ (b), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 2

Only (b) is a correct inference, so the answer is ②.

## LLM Response

Answer-{④ (b), (c)}.

Rationale 1: 
Statement (a) is false. A claims that the only things that exist are the members of the group, so A does not accept that the group exists. B agrees that the group exists, but does not accept that it is the same as the collection of Alice, Bob, and Charlie. 

Statement (b) is true. B agrees that Samshingi and Checkmate are identical, while C does not. C says that Samshingi continues to exist even if the members change, but C does not accept that Checkmate is the same as Samshingi.

Statement (c) is true. A claims that the group does not exist, so A does not accept that the group continues to exist even if a new member replaces Alice. C says that Samshingi continues to exist even if the members change, which is true.
