(c) The logic of explaining the actions of active agents with intentions and purposes is clearly different from the logic of explaining natural phenomena. (d) We explain people's actions through reasons. (e) An important feature of explaining actions by reasons is that such explanations have a justificatory dimension. For example, (f) when we explain why Cheolsu (Alice) opened the window by saying that he wanted fresh air, it reveals that this was the appropriate action to take in that situation. (g) The normative aspect of action explanations cannot be found in causal explanations such as explaining gas expansion through temperature increase. (i) However, the claim that all the various phenomena in this world can be reduced to mere physical phenomena is unconvincing.
