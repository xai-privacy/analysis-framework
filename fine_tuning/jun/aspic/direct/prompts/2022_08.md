Article 3 (Sexual Child Abuse) Anyone who commits an act of sexual abuse causing sexual humiliation to a child shall be punished by imprisonment for not more than 6 years. <Review Opinions> Alice: Crimes of child abuse are distinct from general violent crimes in that the guardian, who has a duty of care, inflicts harm upon the person to be protected.
