## Original Rationale

**Preliminaries**

Due to differences in polarity, steroid hormones can freely pass through the cell membrane and act directly inside the cell, whereas peptide hormones, which cannot cross the membrane, transmit signals into the cell through surface receptors. The plant hormone brassinolide (BR) has recently been shown to use cell surface receptors and intracellular signaling proteins like peptide hormones because, even though it is a steroid hormone, it cannot cross the cell membrane. Part of an experiment elucidating this has been introduced.

**Statement (a) Rationale**

In the passage, it is stated, "Most steroid hormones have low polarity and therefore easily penetrate the cell membrane and act directly as signaling molecules inside the cell, whereas most peptide hormones have high polarity and cannot penetrate the cell membrane." From this, we can deduce that higher polarity means lower membrane permeability. It is also stated, "Animal steroid hormones can easily cross the cell membrane, but the plant steroid hormone brassinolide (BR) ... is known not to be able to cross the membrane easily." Therefore, "If the difference in membrane permeability between animal and plant steroid hormones is determined only by polarity, then BR would be more polar than animal steroid hormones" is a correct analysis.

**Statement (b) Rationale**

In `<Experiment>`, since B's inhibited growth recovered after Br treatment, we can infer that B has a mutation in the Br biosynthesis gene. Since A's inhibited growth was not restored, we can infer that A has a mutation in the Br receptor gene. Therefore, the experiment does NOT support the hypothesis that "A cannot synthesize Br, and B has a defect in the Br receptor." Thus, (b) is not a correct analysis.

**Statement (c) Rationale**

C shows a growth-inhibited phenotype solely due to a mutation in the signaling protein gene, so BR is being synthesized normally. Therefore, simply applying BR cannot restore C's inhibited growth, so it will show a similar phenotype to A, for whom growth does not recover with BR application. Therefore, "After Br treatment, B and C will show similar phenotypes" is not a correct analysis.

**Answer**

Only (a) is correct, so the answer is ①.

> According to the rationale, only statement (a) is correct.
