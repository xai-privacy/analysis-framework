# Question 2025_10

## Original Question

Company P released product A on August 1, 2023. From August 1, 2023 to August 31, 2023, the product was sold at the fixed price of 15,000 won. Thereafter, without specifying any discount rate, the price was set at 14,500 won from September 1 to September 10, 2023; at 13,500 won from September 11 to September 20, 2023; and at 11,000 won from September 21 to September 30, 2023. From October 1, 2023, Company P advertised a ‘Buy 1 Get 1 Free’ promotion for product A for six months via newspapers and flyers, indicating the sale price of one unit as 15,000 won. On February 1, 2024, regulatory agency Q imposed a fine on Company P on the grounds that the ‘Buy 1 Get 1 Free’ advertisement violated [the Regulation]. Q’s reasoning was: “ⓐ Regardless of the method of sale, consumers consider themselves to be purchasing at a 50% discount compared to the previous transaction price, so the ‘Buy 1 Get 1 Free’ promotion is deemed a discount sale.” [Regulation] Article ○ (Discount Sales) ① When a business entity conducts a discount sale of goods, the discount rate shall be indicated, and the previous transaction price for the 30 days immediately preceding the start of advertising shall be stated. However, if the price changed continuously during those 30 days, the previous transaction price shall be either the lower of (i) the average price for the 30 days, or (ii) the average of the lowest and highest prices within that period. ② Notwithstanding Paragraph 1, where discount sales are conducted consecutively under different conditions, the previous transaction price during the 30 days prior to the first discount sale shall be stated. ③ In case of violation of Paragraph 1 or Article 2, a fine not exceeding 30 million won may be imposed. <question> Which of the following can be correctly inferred from the above? Select all correct statements from <statements>. <statements> (a) If the interpretation is that Company P’s act of indicating 15,000 won as the sale price is solely for the purpose of a giveaway because the discount rate for the ‘Buy 1 Get 1 Free’ promotion was not directly stated, this weakens ⓐ. (b) According to Q, Company P should have indicated the sale price of product A as 13,000 won. (c) If sales involving discounts without indicating the discount rate are also regarded as discount sales, then even if Company P’s ‘Buy 1 Get 1 Free’ event is acknowledged as a discount sale, the fact that Company P indicated 15,000 won as the sale price in this event does not violate [the Regulation]. <choices> ① (a) ② (b) ③ (a), (c) ④ (b), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c).

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 3

Only (a) and (c) from the statements are correct inferences, so the answer is ③.

## LLM Response

Answer-①. Rationale 1. If the interpretation is that Company P’s act of indicating 15,000 won as the sale price is solely for the purpose of a giveaway because the discount rate for the ‘Buy 1 Get 1 Free’ promotion was not directly stated, then this weakens Article ○ (Discount Sales) ①, which states that the discount rate shall be indicated. 2. This weakens the applicability of the regulation to the ‘Buy 1 Get 1 Free’ promotion, since the regulation assumes that the discount rate was directly stated.
