# Question 2022_30

## Original Question

A: Due to future technological changes such as digital transformation, opportunities to earn income through jobs may decrease. In order to prepare for this, it is necessary to introduce a basic income system which pays the same amount unconditionally to every citizen without any individual conditions. Even if welfare benefits are concentrated on those who are selected as being in social risk, there may still be blind spots, and there is also considerable friction caused by the selection process. Universal provision can alleviate these problems and provide practical help to everyone without any blind spots.B: Although basic income appears to solve poverty by providing a certain amount to everyone, if the basic income is paid only within the limits permitted by the government’s fiscal situation, the benefit level will be too low to effectively address the blind spots.C: If existing welfare systems are reorganized and the public sector is reformed, it would be possible to secure the financial resources for basic income. If everyone receives a certain benefit, polarization will be alleviated. Accordingly, tax resistance will decrease, public support for redistributive policies will increase, and a virtuous cycle is expected in which overall welfare funding will also expand.D: Strengthening the existing social safety net, such as the Basic Livelihood Security System for supporting the living expenses of the poor or employment insurance to compensate for income loss from unemployment, is a more effective alternative for addressing polarization than basic income. <question> Which of the following evaluations regarding the above debate is correct? Choose as it is from the <statements>.<statements> (a) Research findings that more jobs will disappear than be newly created due to the spread of AI with the development of the Fourth Industrial Revolution weaken Alice’s argument.(b) If there is a survey result that emergency disaster relief funds temporarily provided to all citizens to support those suffering from national disasters did not affect the business closure rate among self-employed people, then Bob’s argument is weakened.(c) If, when basic income is provided with funds obtained by integrating existing welfare systems, the income share of the highest income decile significantly decreases compared to the lowest income decile, then Charlie's argument is strengthened and Dave’s is weakened. <choices> ① (a) ② (c) ③ (a), (b) ④ (b), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 2

As only (c) in <choices> is a correct evaluation, the answer is ②.

## LLM Response

Answer-②

 Rationale 1. Since Charlie's argument is strengthened when the income share of the highest income decile significantly decreases compared to the lowest income decile, and Dave's argument is weakened under these conditions, the correct evaluation of the argument can be derived from the result of this comparison. 2. When the income share of the highest income decile significantly decreases compared to the lowest income decile, this means that a basic income system can effectively alleviate the polarization of income, which is a key point of Charlie's argument.
