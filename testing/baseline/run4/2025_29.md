# Question 2025_29

## Original Question

Various studies on gender inequality in workplaces with a high proportion of men demonstrate that women receive relatively fewer opportunities and lower evaluations compared to men, even when accounting for their actual abilities and achievements. This structural situation faced by women makes it difficult for them to fully exhibit their abilities on teams carrying out projects that require creativity. Creative projects generate synergy when team members gather and engage in active interaction together. However, if women are restricted in their opportunities to demonstrate creativity during team activities, or if their creative expressions themselves are undervalued, women will not be able to fully realize their capabilities within team activities. In this context, “ⓐWomen will display greater creativity in environments where they work separately from men rather than environments where they work together with men.” To test this, the following <Experiment> was conducted.<Experiment>Forty male singers and forty female singers were recruited, and then paired at random to form 40 mixed-gender duets. Of the 40 pairs, 20 were randomly assigned to the control group; each pair recorded the same song in the studio together, accompanied by musicians. The remaining 20 pairs were assigned to the experimental group: the male singer in each pair recorded the same song in the studio with the musicians, while the female singer, alone in the studio, listened to the recording of the male singer and the musicians and recorded her own part, thereby completing the song. There were five musicians in total, all of whom were men, and the same musicians played in all 40 duets. After the recordings were complete, a panel of experts with equal numbers of men and women listened to the songs and evaluated the creative expression of the male and female singers by scoring them. <question> Which of the following in <statements> correctly evaluates the <Experiment>? <statements> (a) If the female singers’ scores were higher in the experimental group than in the control group, ⓐ is supported.(b) If the total score of all female singers was higher than that of all male singers, ⓐ is supported.(c) If the female singers’ scores in the control group were slightly lower than those of the male singers, but in the experimental group the female singers’ scores were higher than those of the male singers, ⓐ is weakened. <choices>① (a) ② (c) ③ (a), (b) ④ (b), (c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 1

Therefore, only (a) provides a correct evaluation, so the answer is ①.

## LLM Response

Answer-③ (a), (b).

Rationale 1. The statement ⓐ suggests that women will display greater creativity in environments where they work separately from men rather than environments where they work together with men. This is exactly what the experiment aimed to test. 

Rationale 2. The experiment compared the performance of female singers in two different settings: with male singers in the same studio and with female singers working alone and listening to the male singer's recording. Therefore, if the female singers' scores were higher in the experimental group than in the control group, it would support statement ⓐ. 

Rationale 3. The statement ⓐ is also supported by the observation that if the total score of all female singers was higher than that of all male singers, it would also support statement ⓐ, as it suggests that female singers performed better in both groups. 

Rationale 4. The experiment did not involve female singers working together with male singers, so the observation that female singers' scores in the control group were slightly lower than those of the male singers is not relevant to the test of statement ⓐ. This observation, therefore, does not weaken statement ⓐ.
