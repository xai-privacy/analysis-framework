# Question 2023_11

## Original Question

[Real Estate Holding Tax Rate under Country X's Tax Law]Real estate price: up to 500 million won, tax rate: 0.5%Real estate price: more than 500 million won up to 1 billion won, tax rate: 1.5%Real estate price: more than 1 billion won up to 2 billion won, tax rate: 2.5%Real estate price: over 2 billion won, tax rate: 3.5%<Situation>Company Alice and Company Bob belong to Group P, while Company Charlie and Company Diane are run by the Q family. Group P is registered as a group in the corporate registry, whereas Q is not registered as a group. According to the current tax law of Country X, real estate held by each company is taxed individually. (Neither P nor Q itself owns any real estate.)<Viewpoints>Viewpoint 1: Taxation should be carried out based on the unit of the economic community. Companies within a single group registered in the corporate registry can be viewed as an economic community. For example, under the current law, if only Alice in Group P owns real estate worth 1 billion won, the total tax amount is different from the case where Alice and Bob each own real estate worth 500 million won. Since this does not reflect their status as an economic community, it is unjust. Taxation should be based on the total value of all real estate held by the companies in Group P, regardless of the individual real estate values owned by each company. In other words, an aggregated taxation should be imposed on the real estate holdings of the companies within Group P.Viewpoint 2: While it is desirable for taxation to be based on the unit of the economic community, if aggregate taxation of real estate holdings is imposed only on groups registered in the corporate registry, certain problems arise. For example, since Company Charlie and Company Diane, run by the Q family, are not registered as a group in the corporate registry, their real estate holdings will not be aggregated for tax purposes. When the combined real estate value of companies belonging to P and Q is identical, both have the characteristics of an economic community, yet P will end up paying more taxes than Q, leading to an unfair outcome. Therefore, it would be better to tax each company individually according to the current tax law, regardless of group registration status. <question> Which of the analyzes below is/are correct according to the information above? Choose all that apply from the <statements>.<statements>(a) If the total real estate value of the companies in P does not exceed 500 million won, the total tax amount will not differ whether taxation is according to Viewpoint 1 or Viewpoint 2.(b) If the total real estate value of the companies in P exceeds 2 billion won, there is no situation in which the total tax amount is the same when taxation is according to Viewpoint 1 and when it is according to Viewpoint 2.(c) If the tax law is revised to treat substantive economic entities like Q as groups registered in the corporate registry, Viewpoint 2 would not object to aggregate real estate holding tax on P. <choices>① (a) ② (b) ③ (a), (c) ④ (b),(c) ⑤ (a), (b), (c)

## Original Answer

**Answer choice:** 3

Since only (a) and (c) are correct analyses, the answer is ③.

## LLM Response

Answer-{5}. 1. If the total real estate value of the companies in P exceeds 500 million won, the total tax amount may be the same when taxation is according to Viewpoint 1 and when it is according to Viewpoint 2. 2. If the tax law is revised to treat substantive economic entities like Q as groups registered in the corporate registry, Viewpoint 2 would object to aggregate real estate holding tax on P. 3. If the total real estate value of the companies in P exceeds 2 billion won, the situation in which the total tax amount is the same when taxation is according to Viewpoint 1 and when it is according to Viewpoint 2 is impossible.
